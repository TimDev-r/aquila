# Aquila – 3-minute demo script

**Before you go on stage**
- [ ] Laptop on the charger, notifications off, sound on and loud enough for the room.
- [ ] App open in a fresh tab on the **Learn** page. Do one click anywhere on the page first, so the browser allows audio.
- [ ] **Demo panel** (gear icon) → **Refill 5 hearts**.
- [ ] Backup video open in a second tab, paused at 0:00.
- [ ] Practise the lucky trade once in Replay mode, so you know roughly when the price is green.

---

## 1. The problem – 20 s (0:00–0:20)

**Screen:** Learn page, with the headline "Trade with a reason, not a hunch."

> "Trading apps make it easy to gamble and hard to learn. In the EU, brokers have to publish how many retail accounts lose money, and it's commonly over 70%. One lucky win can teach you exactly the wrong lesson. Aquila is a Duolingo for trading, and its AI coach grades your *reasoning*, not your profit."

## 2. A lesson – 30 s (0:20–0:50)

**Clicks**
1. On **Learn**, click lesson 4, **The Stop Loss**.
2. Tap through one or two intro cards and read one aloud.
3. On the question *"You buy at \$50 because support sits at \$48. Where does your stop belong?"*, pick **"At \$30, to give it lots of room."** on purpose.
4. Point at the explanation and the lost heart.

> "Short lessons, real numbers. I'll get this one wrong on purpose. Aquila explains why: the stop belongs just below the level that proves you wrong, not miles away. Wrong answers cost a heart, like in Duolingo."

## 3. The lucky trade – 50 s (0:50–1:40)

**Clicks**
1. Click **Trade** in the navigation.
2. Switch the toggle from **Live** to **Replay**. The scenario **"Ethereum at a crossroads"** (`demo-lucky-win`) is selected by default.
3. In the order panel, keep **Long ↑**, leave **Stop loss** and **Take profit** empty.
4. Type the thesis: **`feels like it will go up, I have a good feeling`**
5. Click **Buy ETH**.
6. Set the speed to **4x**.
7. When the open position is in profit, click **Close**.

> "Now the classic beginner trade. Live Binance data or Replay mode with real historical charts – I'll use Replay so it's the same every time. I go long, no stop loss, and my reason is… 'it feels like it will go up, I have a good feeling.' Note that Aquila won't even let you trade without writing a reason. Let's speed it up… and I'm in profit. Close it. Most apps would throw confetti now."

## 4. The verdict – 30 s (1:40–2:10)

**Screen:** the trade review dialog opens. Point at the **Gemma 4** badge, the score and the **lucky** verdict. Let Aquila's voice finish speaking.

> "Instead, Gemma 4 reviews the reasoning. I made money, but the verdict is *lucky*: no stop loss, no plan, just a feeling. And Aquila tells you why, out loud, in its own voice from ElevenLabs."

If the voice doesn't play, click the **replay** button in the dialog once.

## 5. Chart Vision – 30 s (2:10–2:40)

**Clicks**
1. Close the dialog and click **Chart Vision**.
2. Click **Try a sample chart**.
3. Point at the trend, key levels and the quick-check question.

> "You can also show Aquila any chart. Gemma 4 is multimodal, so it reads the image: trend, pattern, key levels. Then it turns what it sees into a quick question, so you learn instead of copying."

## 6. Close – 20 s (2:40–3:00)

**Screen:** back to the Learn page.

> "Aquila runs on Gemma 4, an open-weight model, with prompts you can read in our repo. The voice is ElevenLabs, the app was built in Lovable, and the whole project is open source under MIT. Watch. Think. Then strike. Thank you!"

---

## Backup plan if the Wi-Fi fails – 20 s

Say this and keep going:

> "The Wi-Fi is down, which is a nice test: Aquila is built to keep working offline."

1. **Trading:** stay in **Replay** mode. It uses built-in historical data and needs no network.
2. **Review:** the trade review still opens with the **Offline coach** badge instead of Gemma 4. Say: "Without internet you get the built-in coach; online, Gemma 4 does this live." The voice falls back to the browser's voice.
3. **If anything freezes:** switch to the second tab and play the **backup video** from where you are.
