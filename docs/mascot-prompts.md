# Aquila Eagle Mascot Prompts (Top Hat & Cane Series)

Consistent character prompts based on the chosen Aquila mascot (navy suit, white shirt, red tie, white spiky head feathers, yellow beak).

## Important: Getting Transparent Backgrounds
AI generators output on solid backgrounds.
1. Use the prompts below with: `isolated on pure solid white background, zero shadows, sticker cutout style`.
2. Open Canva, upload the image, and click **Edit Photo -> BG Remover** (or drop it into [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg)) for an instant 100% transparent PNG.
