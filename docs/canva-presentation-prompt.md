# Concise 3-Slide Canva Presentation Prompt

Optimized for a 3-minute hackathon pitch with a live product demonstration.

```text
Create a high-impact, minimalist 3-slide pitch deck for "Aquila: An AI Trading Platform". 
Design style: Modern dark-mode fintech aesthetic (deep black/charcoal background, gold/amber eagle accents, clean bold white typography, high contrast, clean data infographics).

Slide 1: The Brutal Truth (The Problem)
• Headline: Trading Apps Are Built Like Casinos
• Huge Stat Callout: "70% – 85%"
• Sub-text: Of retail trader accounts lose money (Source: Mandatory EU ESMA & UK FCA broker disclosures).
• Key Contrast Points:
  - Casino apps celebrate lucky gambles with green confetti, reinforcing fatal habits.
  - 90% of account blowups come from emotional FOMO and lack of stop loss discipline.
• Bottom Callout: "When you win on a hunch, you're just one trade away from zero."

Slide 2: The Solution (Duolingo Meets Trading)
• Headline: Aquila — Trade with a Reason, Not a Hunch
• Subtitle: The first trading platform that grades your reasoning, not your profit.
• 3 Core Innovation Cards:
  1. Micro-Learning: Bite-sized interactive lessons with streaks, XP, and hearts.
  2. Thesis-Enforced Paper Trading: The Buy button is locked until you write a real thesis.
  3. AI Reasoning Verdict: AI evaluates your decision process—penalizing lucky unmanaged trades even if they make money.
• Visual Transition Callout: "Let's see it live on the market ➔" (Bridge to live demo)

Slide 3: The Tech Stack & Prize Tracks (Closing Slide)
• Headline: Built in 4 Hours · 100% Open Source
• 4 Hackathon Prize Badges:
  - Best Use of Gemma 4: Open-weight AI evaluating trade reasoning & multimodal chart vision.
  - Best Use of ElevenLabs: Low-latency voice coach bringing Aquila to life.
  - Best Use of Lovable: Full-stack TanStack Start + Tailwind architecture.
  - Best Open-Source AI Project: MIT licensed with transparent prompt contracts.
• Motto / Mic Drop: "Watch. Think. Then strike."
• Footer: GitHub Repository | Live App
```
